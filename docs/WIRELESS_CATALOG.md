# Outdoor transceiver catalog

Checked 2026-10-13 against manufacturer listings. Stock and regional availability vary. This is a curated expansion, not every current SKU. No switches, controllers, passive antennas or control modules are catalog products.

2.4/5/6 GHz profiles only. 11/24/60 GHz radios are outside the current 1–10 GHz engine and are not selectable. Beamwidths are editable provisional approximations; published gains are per antenna, not summed across chains. TX20 dBm/RX−85 dBm remain planning assumptions. Connectorized radios start at 0 dBi with an explicit external-antenna warning and unverified pairing until gain is configured.

Proprietary families are isolated. Same-band standard Wi-Fi association is conditional; transparent bridging depends on drivers and configuration. No throughput, beamforming, capacity, stock or availability guarantee. Subscriber pairs require explicit PTP support.
